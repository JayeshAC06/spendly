Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your financial information is personal. This Privacy Policy explains what data Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

01 Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and access times, used to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details or connect to your financial institutions.

02 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Create and manage your Spendly account.
  • Store, display and summarise your expenses.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • Contact you about important account or policy changes.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03 Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database and is accessible only through your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never visible to us. We keep your data for as long as your account is active.

You can request deletion of your account at any time, after which your personal and expense data will be permanently removed.

04 Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting and font services, to deliver the app. These providers only receive the minimum information required to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

We do not share your expense data with third parties except where required by law.

05 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to access, correct or delete your data, please reach out to the Spendly team. We aim to respond to all privacy requests promptly.
